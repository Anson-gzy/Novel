# Chapter 1: "Keep a Place" — Notes

## 1. Pre-Flight Planning

- **Core Event:** Naim is on a bus heading to the city. Ryan got on at the last stop. The chapter resolves the film's ambiguous ending: this IS Ryan, not the entity, because there are other people on the bus. They arrive in the city late at night. They have no plan, no money beyond what Ryan grabbed, and nowhere to go. They find a 24-hour McDonald's and sit until morning.
- **Emotional Transition:** Terror and numbness from the film's climax → cautious relief that Ryan is real → exhaustion → the quiet panic of "now what?"
- **Timeline:** D+0 evening to D+1 early morning. Bus ride is 3-4 hours. Arrive in the city around midnight.
- **Side Character Planning:** No side characters in this chapter. Just Naim and Ryan, and the anonymous passengers on the bus.
- **Chekhov's Gun Plan:**
  - Plant: Naim's lighter (he still has it from fighting the entity at the mill). He flicks it open and closed on the bus. It's comfort now.
  - Plant: Ryan's phone (battery almost dead, voicemail still set to "This is Ryan. Leave a message.")
  - Plant: Jessica's elastic band (Naim picked one up at the hospital, keeps it on his wrist without thinking about why)
  - Extend: The entity is not present in this chapter, but its absence creates tension. Every time Ryan does something slightly off, Naim's fear spikes.

## 2. Post-Flight Overview

- **Actual Events:** Naim rides the bus to Sydney with Ryan. He can't be certain Ryan is real but relies on the rule that the entity can't appear around other people. They arrive at Central Station around midnight, walk to a 24-hour McDonald's on George Street. They establish the watch system: one sleeps, one watches. Ryan takes first shift. Naim lies down but doesn't actually sleep.
- **Hook:** Ryan says "I'll know" when asked how he'll tell real-Naim from the entity. But Naim knows the entity gets better. The question of recognition, of trust, hangs unresolved.
- **Foreshadowing:** The lighter as comfort object. The elastic band from the hospital. The watch system that can't work forever. Arlene's line about fear echoing in Naim's head.
