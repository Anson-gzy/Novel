## Chapter Summaries

### Chapter 1: "Keep a Place"
**Summary:** Naim rides a bus from their small town to Sydney with Ryan, who got on at the last stop. The entire ride, Naim can't be fully certain this is the real Ryan, but holds onto the rule that the entity can't appear when other people are present. He watches Ryan sleep, cataloguing details, testing reality against the dread. They arrive at Central Station after midnight with sixty dollars and no plan. They walk to a 24-hour McDonald's on George Street and establish a watch system: Ryan takes first shift while Naim tries to sleep. Naim lies down but can't close his eyes without seeing the mill, the entity, the fire. Key items planted: Naim's lighter (comfort object), Ryan's dead phone, an elastic band Naim took from the hospital, and Arlene's words about fear echoing in his mind. The chapter ends with Naim's eyes closed but not sleeping, and the quiet understanding between them that this arrangement can't hold.
