# Leviticus: Strange Fire — Character Files

## Main Characters

### Naim
- **Age/Role:** 16-17, high school student
- **Appearance:** Not specified in detail in the film. Middle Eastern heritage (name suggests Lebanese/Arabic background). His mother Arlene is white Australian, suggesting mixed heritage.
- **Core Personality:** Observant, emotionally honest to the point of vulnerability, stubborn when it matters. Says sorry too much. Braver than he thinks.
- **Core Values:** Honesty, loyalty. He told Rod and Jacky about Ryan and Hunter because he was scared, not malicious, but he carries the guilt.
- **Greatest Fear:** Being alone. Both literally (the entity comes when you're alone) and emotionally (abandoned by his mother, losing Ryan).
- **Fatal Flaw:** He freezes. When the fear is too big, he locks up instead of acting. The paralysis is what the entity exploits.
- **Inner Desire:** To stop being afraid of what he feels. To have his mother see him without flinching.
- **Background:** Moved to a small religious town with his mother Arlene after his father Tom's death. Arlene found religion; Naim found Ryan. His mother took him to a "deliverance healer" for conversion therapy, which cursed him with the entity.
- **Base Routine (sequel):** Wakes early, checks that Ryan is still there. Goes to school. Stays in public spaces. Never alone after dark.
- **Idle Animations:** Flicks a lighter open and closed without lighting it. Checks his phone even when nobody has texted. Picks at the skin around his thumbnails.

### Ryan
- **Age/Role:** 16-17, high school student
- **Appearance:** White Australian. Physical, impulsive, moves like someone used to taking up space. Swears constantly.
- **Core Personality:** Loud on the surface, deeply private underneath. Uses aggression as a shield. Loyal in ways he can't articulate. When scared, he gets angry. When angry, he gets quiet.
- **Core Values:** Protection. He'll take a hit for someone before he'll say something kind. Actions over words.
- **Greatest Fear:** That the people he cares about will get hurt because of what he is.
- **Fatal Flaw:** He pushes people away to protect them, which creates the exact isolation the entity needs.
- **Inner Desire:** To stop performing. To be the version of himself he is when he's alone with Naim, but everywhere.
- **Background:** From the same small town. Was secretly involved with Hunter (Rod's son) before Naim arrived. After the healer's ritual, he was cursed too. Hunter died. Ryan blames himself. His parents are complicit in the town's religious culture. He stole Izzie's parents' car to escape the ambush.
- **Base Routine (sequel):** Sleeps late, stays up late. Checks locks, windows, exits. Moves through the city like he's mapping escape routes.
- **Idle Animations:** Cracks knuckles one at a time. Tilts his head when he's thinking, like a dog hearing a frequency. Chews the inside of his cheek.

## Supporting Characters

### Arlene (Naim's mother)
- **Basic Info:** Late 30s-40s. Single mother. Found religion after her husband Tom's death. Moved to the small town to "belong." Took Naim to the deliverance healer out of fear and love.
- **Narrative Function:** The antagonist who isn't a villain. She represents the system that harms queer kids through love, not hate. Her arc is about confronting what her "protection" actually did.
- **Base Routine:** Works, attends church, calls Naim and hangs up.
- **Idle Animations:** Makes food nobody asked for. Organizes things that are already organized. Sits in the car without starting it.

### Jessica
- **Basic Info:** Young woman, late teens or early 20s. Lives at Murryville Hospital to never be alone. Snaps elastic bands on her wrist as a coping mechanism. Was subjected to the healer's ritual along with her friend Marnie, who died at the pool.
- **Narrative Function:** Oracle figure. She's lived with the entity longest. She knows its rules better than anyone.
- **Base Routine:** Hospital grounds. Never alone. Elastic bands.
- **Idle Animations:** Snaps elastic band. Counts people in the room. Watches doors.

### The Healer (unnamed)
- **Basic Info:** Old man. Performs "deliverance healing" rituals in small religious communities. References Leviticus 10 (the sons of Aaron, strange fire). Uses a lighter as part of the ritual.
- **Narrative Function:** The source. Appears late in the story. Turns out to be a victim himself, perpetuating the cycle.
- **Base Routine:** Nomadic. Moves between small towns.
- **Idle Animations:** Mutters scripture. Holds a lighter but doesn't light it.

### Rod (Pastor)
- **Basic Info:** 40s-50s. Pastor of the town church. Hunter's father. Arranged the healer's visit. After Hunter's death, broken by grief.
- **Narrative Function:** Represents the institutional failure. His search for Ryan and Naim in the sequel is driven by guilt, not malice.

### Izzie
- **Basic Info:** Hunter's sister or close family friend. Betrayed Naim and Ryan by luring them to an ambush. Her father (Rod) left the family after Hunter's death.
- **Narrative Function:** Represents the community's complicity. She blamed Naim for Hunter's death.
