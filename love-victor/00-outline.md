### Chapter 01: Ground Level
- **POV:** Victor
- **Time Budget:** D+0, Winter Carnival night, from the Ferris wheel coming down to late night at home.
- **Must Happen:** Victor and Benji get off the wheel together. The friends see them. Benji confirms he is not going to Loomis Chaffee. Benji's mom is waiting in the parking lot. Benji sets the first rule: slow, and his recovery comes first. Victor goes home and tells his family.
- **Cast:** Victor, Benji, Felix, Mia, Andrew, Lake, Lucy, Benji's mom, Isabel, Armando, Pilar, Adrian ｜ mentioned: Mr. Campbell, Nick, Benji's sponsor, Simon.
- **Action Line:** The wheel stops; the operator lets them off. Felix, Mia, Andrew, Lake and Lucy have seen everything and react in character (Felix loudest). Mia reminds everyone she leaves next week. Benji walks Victor toward the lot and says he told his parents he's staying at Creekwood; they aren't happy, but he chose. His mom is waiting in the car; she's polite and careful with Victor. Before he gets in, Benji says he can't do big declarations right now; he needs to take it one day at a time, and Victor can't be the thing that holds him together. They agree to talk tomorrow. Victor finds his family; the Salazars already know something happened. At home, Pilar asks about Benji and says her own thing with Felix is "not back together, just not broken." Late night, Benji texts that he's home and called his sponsor.
- **Ending Hook:** Victor lies awake with Benji's text on his chest, realizing he doesn't know what "slow" looks like.
- **POV Boundary Reminder:** Victor did not hear what Benji's parents said in their "long talk"; he only knows what Benji tells him.

### Chapter 02: Seven Days
- **POV:** Victor
- **Time Budget:** D+1 to D+2, weekend after the carnival.
- **Must Happen:** Victor helps Mia pack for Palo Alto. Mia and Victor talk about Andrew and long distance, and about Benji. Victor and Benji have their first "slow" phone call.
- **Cast:** Victor, Mia, Andrew ｜ phone: Benji ｜ mentioned: Mia's dad, Veronica, the baby brother.
- **Action Line:** Mia's house is half boxes. Victor is there to help and to say goodbye in pieces. Andrew shows up with a plan for visits that is too detailed; Mia lets him have it. Mia asks Victor what "slow" means with Benji and he can't answer. That night Benji calls; they talk about nothing, and it's the best conversation they've had in months.
- **Ending Hook:** Benji says he has a meeting Monday and asks Victor not to wait outside.
- **POV Boundary Reminder:** Victor doesn't know what Benji says in meetings.

### Chapter 03: Day Count
- **POV:** Victor
- **Time Budget:** D+3, Monday.
- **Must Happen:** First school day after the carnival and the award. Benji goes to his meeting; Victor doesn't go. Benji tells Victor his sponsor's rule for him: Victor is not his fixer.
- **Cast:** Victor, Benji, Felix, Rahim, Lake ｜ mentioned: Benji's sponsor, Nick, Coach.
- **Action Line:** Creekwood is buzzing about the "Gay Award" speech and the Ferris wheel. Nick passes Victor in the hall and is decent about it. Victor waits for Benji's text after the meeting and makes himself not drive over. After school, Benji explains the rule. Victor agrees and doesn't fully understand it.
- **Ending Hook:** Victor realizes he's already been counting Benji's sober days in his head.
- **POV Boundary Reminder:** Only what Benji chooses to share about his recovery.

### Chapter 04: Salazar Electricians
- **POV:** Victor
- **Time Budget:** D+4 to D+6.
- **Must Happen:** Armando and Isabel's new business has its first rough week. Victor sees the money stress and keeps it from Benji and friends.
- **Cast:** Victor, Armando, Isabel, Pilar, Adrian, Felix.
- **Action Line:** The kitchen table becomes an office. A job falls through; Isabel covers with a smile. Felix offers help nobody can use. Victor offers to pick up more hours and his parents say no.
- **Ending Hook:** Victor notices he's doing what he always did: being the rock so nobody worries.
- **POV Boundary Reminder:** Victor sees the numbers only when his parents let him.
