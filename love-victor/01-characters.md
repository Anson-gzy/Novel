# Character Profiles

> Only decision-relevant facts. Canon source: S1–S3 transcripts. Each chapter appends a `Ch.NN evolution` line.

## Protagonist

### Victor Salazar
- **Age / Role:** Junior at Creekwood High. Moved from Texas to Atlanta a year before the carnival. Varsity basketball. Just received Creekwood's first Bravery Award ("the Gay Award") and gave the speech.
- **Markers:** The Bravery Award. The speech line "being brave means that you aren't afraid to be scared."
- **Wants / Fears Losing:** Wants Benji and a life where he doesn't hide. Fears being the reason Benji falls again (Mr. Campbell's accusation).
- **Fatal Flaw:** Takes care of everyone so nobody has to worry about him ("my rock"); hides his own fear.
- **Hard Background:** Parents Armando and Isabel (reconciled; launching Salazar Electricians). Sister Pilar, brother Adrian. Lives in an apartment building with Felix upstairs. Brasstown is the café where he and Benji worked. Dated Mia (S1), Benji (S1–S3, broke up in S3), Nick (S3, ended kindly at the award ceremony).
- **Voice:** Warm, self-deprecating, a little awkward when nervous; stammers on the important words.
- **Floor:** Never outs anyone; never lies to Benji about what he feels.
- **Evolution:**
  - Ch.01 evolution: Back with Benji after the wheel; agrees to go "slow" without knowing how; holds back "I love you."

## Supporting Cast

### Benji Campbell
- **Role:** Victor's boyfriend again as of the Ferris wheel. Recovering alcoholic: has a sponsor, went to rehab, relapsed during S3.
- **Relationship to Victor:** First love. Chose Victor on the wheel: "I want us to be together… but I still have to work on myself. Like… a lot."
- **Hard Background:** Aunt is dean at Loomis Chaffee (Connecticut); his parents wanted him there. After a long talk he told them "you're not my trigger. They are."
- **Voice:** Calm, dry, honest to the point of bluntness.
- **Floor:** Recovery comes before the relationship.
- **Evolution:**
  - Ch.01 evolution: Staying at Creekwood, not Loomis Chaffee. Sets the terms: no big declarations, recovery first, Victor isn't what holds him together. Called his sponsor, Dale, that night.

### Felix Westen
- **Role:** Victor's best friend, upstairs neighbor. "Velix" friend-aversary is the carnival day.
- **Hard Background:** His mom has struggled with mental health; the Salazars became his second family. Broke up with Pilar; apologized to her at the carnival and was pulled into the Salazar family photo. Almost restarted with Lake; they agreed it wouldn't be brave.
- **Voice:** Whimsical, over-talks, huge heart.
- **Evolution:**
  - Ch.01 evolution: Saw the Ferris wheel; kept Victor's secret from the Salazars ("your story to tell").

### Pilar Salazar
- **Role:** Victor's younger sister. Felix's ex.
- **Voice:** Sharp, sarcastic, loyal underneath.
- **Evolution:**
  - Ch.01 evolution: Tells Victor she and Felix are "not back together, just not broken"; he's family.
