# Chapter 16: Go Find Him — Notes

## 1. Pre-Flight Planning
- **Core Event:** Justin mentions accumulate. Zack recognizes Andrew's growing feelings. Tries indirect approaches. Eventually gives Andrew explicit permission to pursue Justin. Andrew texts Justin. Zack sits with what he did.
- **Emotional State Transition:** Growing recognition → indirect attempts → the decision → the weight of having done it → quiet, private grief
- **Timeline Budget:** D+14 to D+50. Late September through October.

## 2. Post-Flight Overview
- **Actual Events:** Zack tracks the Justin mentions across weeks of conversations. He notes the specificity — Andrew describes Justin's honesty, his face doing things before his brain can stop it. Zack tries indirect approaches: "You've been hanging out with Justin a lot." Andrew responds neutrally. The conversation goes awkward, recovers, repeats. Zack recognizes the pattern: Andrew has feelings for Justin that he won't act on because of loyalty to Zack. On a Saturday phone call, Zack tells Andrew plainly: "If there's someone you want to spend time with, you should." Andrew admits: "There's someone. His name is Justin." Zack says "Go find him." Andrew texts Justin. Zack sits alone with the silence.
- **Suspense Hook:** The flashback arc closes. Zack has stepped aside. The door is open for Andrew and Justin. The reader now has the full backstory — the gala, the bracelet, the partner, the permission — and understands the architecture of everything that came before.
- **Foreshadowing Planted:**
  - Zack's permission is the catalyst for Andrew's approach to Justin in Ch. 1-6
  - Andrew's description of Justin: "He says what he's feeling even when he doesn't mean to. He can't help it." — connects to Justin's own self-awareness
  - "Go find him" — the sentence that launches the main plot
  - Andrew texting Justin immediately after permission — the speed reveals the depth of wanting
  - Zack's private grief: "the stepping felt like falling, but the falling felt necessary"

## 3. Review & Logic Sync
- **Actual Timeline:** D+14 to D+50. Covers approximately five weeks (late September through late October). Consistent with Andrew being established at West Valley by the time Marcus observes him in Ch. 8 (D+45).
- **Timeline Conflicts:** None. Zack's permission (~D+50) aligns with when Andrew would have been settled enough to start actively pursuing Justin.
- **Character Evolution:** Zack completes his arc for this phase — from detached observer to connected partner to selfless release. His voice is distinct: warm, measured, slightly formal, honest in the dark. Andrew's multi-faceted nature is further confirmed: the way he describes Justin to Zack reveals an Andrew who is losing control of his usual precision.
