# Chapter 14: One Day, One Night — Notes

## 1. Pre-Flight Planning
- **Core Event:** Trapped for a full day and night. Zack's injury prevents movement. Deep conversation — Andrew about Westbrook invisibility, Zack about parental expectations. Zack realizes he doesn't want Andrew to disappear. Rescue — separation — Andrew grabs Zack's hand. Hospital: Zack hears parents were "pronounced at the scene."
- **Emotional State Transition:** Physical pain + enforced stillness → gradual honesty → unexpected intimacy → quiet realization → violent separation during rescue → devastating knowledge
- **Timeline Budget:** D-90 to D-89. ~24 hours trapped, then hospital.

## 2. Post-Flight Overview
- **Actual Events:** Zack and Andrew are trapped for 18+ hours. The first hour is quiet. Andrew offers his jacket for Zack's injured leg. They begin talking — Andrew describes Westbrook as a place where he "didn't register," where rooms didn't change when he entered. Zack talks about swimming, parental expectations, the plan for his life he didn't choose. They reach deep topics: fears, futures, what their lives would look like. Zack realizes he doesn't want Andrew to disappear after rescue. He doesn't say it. Rescue comes through a wall — loud, fast, medical teams. Andrew reaches for Zack's hand before they're separated. In the hospital, under pre-op, Zack overhears: Andrew's parents were "pronounced at the scene."
- **Suspense Hook:** Zack goes under anesthesia knowing Andrew will find out the truth about his parents. The last thing he thinks about is Andrew's face when the real truth arrives.
- **Foreshadowing Planted:**
  - Andrew's Westbrook description: "I'd walk in and the room stayed exactly the same. Like I was made of the same material as the walls." — explains his reinvention motivation
  - Zack's swimming/parental expectations — establishes his own cage
  - "Terrible implies someone is doing something to you. Nobody was doing anything." — Andrew's core wound
  - The hand-grab during rescue — first physical connection, echoes across the relationship
  - "Pronounced at the scene" — dramatic irony payoff
