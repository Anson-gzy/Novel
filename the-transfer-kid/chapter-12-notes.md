# Chapter 12 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** A regular Thursday lunch. The table is at its low hum, Andrew unmentioned, Marcus and Justin both carrying private weight. Without warning, Justin shouts Marcus's name across the cafeteria and punches him. Marcus goes down. The chapter ends on the floor of the cafeteria with Justin standing over him, eyes wet.
- **Carries from Previous:** Marcus's undisclosed feelings for Andrew; Justin's undisclosed deterioration; the friend group's collective avoidance of the Andrew topic; Andrew's revelation that he has a partner.
- **Hook:** Cliffhanger. Marcus is on the ground. Justin is standing over him. The cafeteria is frozen. No explanation yet for why.

## Chapter Notes
- **Hook:** Maximum tension cliffhanger. The reader doesn't know *why* Justin punched Marcus. Multiple possibilities: Did Justin find out Marcus liked Andrew? Did Justin find out about the phone number secret? Did something else happen off-page? The ambiguity is the hook.
- **Next chapter preview:** The aftermath — likely needs to shift back to Justin POV to reveal what triggered the punch. The friend group's reaction. Potential consequences (school discipline, friendship fracture).
- **Foreshadowing planted:**
  - Justin's eyes being "wet, not crying" — the punch cost him something; this isn't rage, it's overflow
  - Marcus's reflection on "the architecture of everything that had changed" — he's aware of the structural shift but not that he's about to become the focal point of it
  - The cafeteria as a public stage — the most public possible venue for a private eruption, which will have social consequences
  - "His hand was still half-closed" — Justin didn't commit to making a fist; the punch was impulsive, not practiced
  - Samira and Dylan's immediate reactions — the group witnesses the fracture in real time
  - Marcus noticing Justin was "present but not participating" — he clocked Justin's state but couldn't predict the direction of the explosion
  - The "concentric circles" of dying conversations — the entire school now has a story to tell, which connects to the Instagram gossip ecosystem from Ch. 1
  - Marcus on the floor looking up at Justin — a complete inversion of the power dynamic; Marcus, who watches everyone from a position of analytical distance, is now the one being looked down at
