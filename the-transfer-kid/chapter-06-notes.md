# Chapter 6 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** Andrew calls Justin (first phone call), picks him up in a BMW, and brings him to a penthouse apartment that shatters every assumption Justin had about Andrew's situation. Justin meets Andrew's brother Ethan, who is clearly running something — not a struggling college dropout. They hang out, listen to music, and Justin takes the train home in a quietly devastated state.
- **Carries from Previous:** Justin's paralysis at the end of Ch. 5 — dressed, ready, frozen. Andrew resolves it by calling, which Justin never expected.
- **Hook:** Justin is home, lying on his bed, and the world he lives in feels smaller than it did before he walked into Andrew's. He is, quietly, in much worse shape than before.

## Chapter Notes
- **Hook:** The final image — Justin on his own bed, in his own room, unable to sleep — mirrors the end of Ch. 5 (also on his bed, also frozen), but the emotional register has shifted from anticipation to something heavier. He's been let in, and being let in made the distance worse.
- **Next chapter preview:** Marcus POV. The friend group discusses Andrew asking for Justin's number. Justin arrives and the topic switches.
- **Foreshadowing planted:**
  - Andrew calling instead of texting — demonstrates intentionality and a comfort with directness that contrasts with Justin's overthinking
  - "I asked around" — deliberately vague about how he got Justin's number; this will land differently when Marcus's POV reveals the truth in Ch. 7
  - The BMW and penthouse — the transfer was never about money, which opens the real mystery: what actually happened at Westbrook Prep?
  - Ethan (Andrew's brother) — young, competent, running something real. Not the struggling guardian Justin imagined. Reframes the entire family dynamic.
  - The face-down photo frames in Andrew's room — deliberately hidden, not removed. Someone he doesn't want to look at but can't throw away.
  - Andrew's playlist — first deliberate act of sharing (bracelet and "I trust you" were accidental leaks; this was chosen)
  - "See you Tuesday" — Andrew has already mapped when they'll next see each other, mirroring Justin's earlier pattern-tracking
  - Justin's "smaller world" feeling — the class gap between them is now visible and felt, adding a new layer of vulnerability to the dynamic
  - The sunset from the platform — Andrew knowing about it suggests he's taken this route, seen this view, thought about it. A small piece of interiority disguised as practical advice.
