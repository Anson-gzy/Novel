# Chapter 3 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** Justin follows Andrew on Instagram and begins unconsciously tracking his daily patterns. He learns Andrew lives with an older brother.
- **Carries from Previous:** Justin's active curiosity after "I trust you"; the question of who Andrew really is
- **Hook:** Justin knows more about Andrew than he has any reason to know — and he wants more.

## Chapter Notes
- **Hook:** Justin's quiet accumulation of knowledge about Andrew — the hallway, the timing, the brother, the Instagram feed — crosses from assigned obligation into personal investment. He can't deny it anymore.
- **Next chapter preview:** Andrew greets Justin unprompted; a Tiffany bracelet opens the first real conversation; Andrew invites Justin over.
- **Foreshadowing planted:**
  - Instagram follow as the first deliberate reach toward Andrew (initiating digital proximity)
  - The 8-month gap in Andrew's posts — aligns with the summer his parents died
  - The single photo of an empty hallway — emotional residue Andrew doesn't express in person
  - Pattern-tracking behavior — Justin's attention is becoming obsessive without him naming it
  - Andrew's older brother (22–23, dropped out to take custody) — new backstory thread
  - Justin refusing to discuss Andrew with friends — protective instinct emerging
  - Samira's perceptiveness ("you're never just curious") — friends see through Justin before he sees through himself
