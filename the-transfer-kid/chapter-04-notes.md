# Chapter 4 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** Andrew greets Justin unprompted, reveals a Tiffany bracelet with a mysterious origin, and they have their first real conversation. Andrew invites Justin to his place after school.
- **Carries from Previous:** Justin's covert attention and growing curiosity; the gap between Andrew's public blankness and private complexity
- **Hook:** Justin said yes instantly — his body answered before his brain caught up. He doesn't know what that means.

## Chapter Notes
- **Hook:** The impulsive "yes" — Justin's response to the invitation bypassed every defense mechanism he has. The sweating hands are the first physical signal he can't intellectualize away.
- **Next chapter preview:** Justin's friends tease him at lunch; he spirals into doubt about whether the invitation is special or generic; he gets ready and freezes.
- **Foreshadowing planted:**
  - Andrew's voluntary "hey" — first unprompted initiation of contact (the wall opening from inside)
  - The Tiffany bracelet — "someone who isn't around anymore" (could be parents, could be someone else; plants a mystery that will pay off later)
  - Andrew's near-smile — "the space where a smile used to live" (the persona is real but incomplete; the old Andrew leaks through)
  - "It'll be fine" echoing "I trust you" — Andrew is building a pattern of quiet affirmation toward Justin specifically
  - Justin's sweating hands — first involuntary physical reaction, crossing from intellectual curiosity into something bodily
  - "The answer had been sitting in my mouth waiting for the question" — Justin's desire is ahead of his awareness
