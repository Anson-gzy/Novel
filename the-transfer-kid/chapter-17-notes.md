- **Emotional State Transition:** Gradual noticing → tracking phone → hearing about Marcus → paranoid math → quiet withdrawal → insomnia

## 2. Post-Flight Overview
- **Actual Events:** Justin notices a "gap where the warmth used to be" in Andrew's behavior. He starts tracking Andrew's phone — face-up more often, typing indicator appearing and disappearing, quick responses to someone else. He doesn't ask who. Through Dylan, he learns Marcus has been "weird" and asking about Andrew. Samira confirms she has a theory but says "it's not my thing to share." Justin runs the math: Andrew pulling away + Marcus paying attention + Samira withholding = Marcus and Andrew, something between them. He doesn't confront anyone. He watches, waits, and gets increasingly bad at sleeping. His "fine" becomes an empty reflex.
- **Suspense Hook:** Justin is trapped in an observation-insomnia loop. The tension is building toward something he can't control or predict.
- **Foreshadowing Planted:**
  - The typing indicator = Andrew texting Zack (dramatic irony: reader knows this now)
  - Justin's "fine" reflex — parallels Marcus's "fine" from Ch. 11
  - Dylan's observation: "Marcus has been weird" — confirms the group noticed Marcus's interest
  - Samira's non-confirmation confirmation — she knows about Marcus's feelings but protects the information
  - Justin's "paranoid math" pointing at Marcus — seeds the wrong deduction of Ch. 19
  - "A different kind of quiet" from Marcus — the reader recognizes this from Ch. 12
