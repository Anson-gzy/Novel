# Chapter 5 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** Justin's friends tease him about Andrew's sudden friendliness; internally Justin spirals into doubt about whether Andrew's attention is specific to him or generic. He gets ready to go to Andrew's place but freezes — no address, no number, no ability to send a message.
- **Carries from Previous:** The impulsive "yes" to Andrew's invitation; the accumulation of private knowledge about Andrew
- **Hook:** Justin is paralyzed — dressed, ready, unmoved. He wants something he can't name and is afraid of what happens if he goes.
