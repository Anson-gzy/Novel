# Chapter 10 Notes

## Chapter Overview
- **Core Event:** Marcus observes the cooling between Justin and Andrew from both sides — Justin's surface-level normalcy hiding something underneath, Andrew's Government-class presence dropping from seventy to sixty percent. Marcus privately concludes that Andrew showed his real self and Justin didn't like what he found. The chapter's secret: Marcus has developed feelings for Andrew that he can't explain and keeps entirely to himself.
- **Carries from Previous:** Marcus's growing frustration with being invisible to Andrew; Justin's defensive reaction at lunch; the friend group's established dynamic.
- **Hook:** The friend group moves around the absence of Andrew's name like furniture that's been removed, and Marcus carries his own secret alongside Justin's.
