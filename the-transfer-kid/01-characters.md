# Character Profiles

## Main Characters

### Justin Herrera (Narrator)
- **Age / Grade:** 16–17, Junior
- **Appearance:** Not heavily described (first-person narrator). Latino surname. Casual dresser.
- **Core Personality:** Sarcastic, socially comfortable, slightly lazy but self-aware. Reluctantly does the right thing when pushed. More observant than he lets on.
- **Core Values:** Loyalty to friends, authenticity, low tolerance for pretension
- **Greatest Fear:** Being trapped in something he didn't choose (whether a role, a feeling, or a responsibility)
- **Fatal Flaw:** Avoidance — he deflects with humor and passivity rather than engaging with things that unsettle him
- **Inner Desire:** To be genuinely known, though he'd never say that out loud
- **Background:** Out as gay — treats it as a background fact, not a defining storyline. Average student (C+ to low B), standard-level classes. Solid friend group. Dad left when Justin was nine; mom works doubles. House is often empty when he gets home. Lives in LA.
- **Ch.27 Evolution:** Received a three-day suspension for punching Marcus. Firmly rejected Andrew's post-facto intellectual explanations outside the east gate, recognizing that Andrew's delayed honesty was self-preservation. Severed all personal and academic ties with Andrew.
- **Ch.28 Evolution:** Returned home to an empty apartment with bruised knuckles and three days of suspension. Learned from Samira that Marcus suffered a soft tissue contusion and stitches but lied to the administration to save him from expulsion. Stares down the crushing realization of his own paranoia and the debt he owes to Marcus.
- **Speech Pattern:** Short sentences, casual contractions, occasional dry humor. Doesn't monologue — thinks in fragments.
- **MBTI:** ISFP

### Andrew Hayes
- **Age / Grade:** 16–17, Junior (transferred at start of this semester)
- **Appearance:** Dark hair, slightly messy in an intentional way. Dark brown eyes. Wears plain, clean clothes (black jacket, white shirt). Nothing flashy. Carries himself with unusual stillness and self-possession.
- **Core Personality:** Presents as confident, bold, slightly untouchable. One-on-one: cold, minimal, unreadable. In a crowd: magnetic. This is a reinvention — at his old school he was isolated with poor relationships.
- **Core Values:** Self-sufficiency, protecting what little connection he has left after losing his family
- **Greatest Fear:** Returning to who he was before — weak, isolated, pitied — or losing Zack Chen
- **Fatal Flaw:** His reinvented persona is a fortress, not a home. He cannot sustain it without eventually cracking. He manages relationships with excessive calculation out of fear of loss.
- **Inner Desire:** Connection, though he's buried it so deep he struggles to manage it honestly
- **Background:** Transferred from Westbrook Preparatory (private school, Brentwood area). Lost both parents over the summer. At his old school: isolated, poor relationships with everyone. Now enrolled in all AP-level courses. Academically exceptional.
- **Ch.25-26 Evolution:** Keeps a physical black leather notebook where he meticulously tracks Zack's habits, recovery notes, and emotional cues. Bared his vulnerabilities in a 24-hour pancake diner, reconciled with Zack, and resolved to set firm boundaries with Justin.
- **Ch.27 Evolution:** Attempted to rationally explain the situation to Justin after the cafeteria brawl; experienced the absolute failure of his intellectual armor when Justin rejected the explanation. Accepted the finality, packed away the gift book, and sought emotional grounding with Zack in Brentwood.
- **Speech Pattern:** Minimal. Functional. One-word or one-sentence responses. Never volunteers information. Speaks like someone who has decided that words are tools, not bridges.
- **MBTI:** INTJ

### Wade Richardson (New Character — Ch.20–24)
- **Age:** 18
- **Appearance:** Half-Asian, sharp jawline, messy hair, effortless style (hoodies, casual clothes). Lives in a modern bluff-side house in Pacific Palisades with floor-to-ceiling glass.
- **Background:** Popular content creator (1.2 million followers on social media). Famous for unfiltered, humorous, physical videos ("I break things", gas station snack reviews, flat-pack furniture outtakes).
- **Core Personality:** Extremely perceptive, direct, physically confident, completely unbothered by pretense. Sees through facades instantly ("noting the gap").
- **Relationship:** In a 2-year non-traditional open relationship with Moon Thompson (editorial/runway model). Briefly connected with Zack (who used the fake handle `justin.k`) over DMs and a Friday night in Palisades. Understood Zack's true nature without forcing him to drop the mask.
- **MBTI:** ESTP

## Supporting Cast

### Dylan
- Justin's close friend. Joker of the group. Teases Justin constantly. Likely the first to notice if Justin starts acting differently around Andrew. Quick-witted, not mean-spirited.

### Samira
- Part of Justin's friend group. Observant, plugged into the Instagram gossip network. More thoughtful than Dylan — notices things but doesn't always comment.
- **Ch.28 Evolution:** Served as the moral compass and practical anchor of the friend group. Dropped off Justin's backpack, ibuprofen, and class assignments; bluntly confronted Justin with the reality of Marcus's protection and demanded genuine accountability without excuses.

### Tyler (TylerMoss)
- Friend of Justin's. Straightforward, says what's on his mind without overthinking it. Asks the obvious questions that others skip past. Not the sharpest reader of subtext, but not oblivious either — he just doesn't complicate things. Reliable presence in the group.
- **Ch.8 Evolution:** Marcus identifies Tyler as the only other "non-performer" in the friend group. Marcus is honest on purpose; Tyler is "honest by accident." Tyler has a talent for accidentally saying the right thing in the wrong words (e.g., comparing Andrew's behavior to posting song lyrics as captions). He's also the only one willing to call Marcus out directly ("you literally gave him Justin's number"), which Marcus respects even if he ignores it.

### Becca (BeccaLin)
- Active in the Instagram chat. Empathetic — she was the one who pointed out Andrew's parents died. Not necessarily close to Justin but in the same orbit.

### Marcus (MarcusW)
- Justin's friend. Second POV character (Chapters 7–12). Blunt, principled, extremely observant. Prefers people who say what they mean. Keeps his word because he gave it, not out of loyalty or affection. Has a dry, understated voice. Sees more than he lets on but doesn't push unless it matters to him. Currently holds the secret that he gave Andrew Justin's number.
- **Ch.10–11 Evolution:** Developed feelings for Andrew — illogical, unexplainable, kept completely to himself. Was directly rejected by Andrew in Government class (Ch. 11). Carries this alongside the phone number secret. Punched by Justin in Ch. 12/19/26 due to Justin's mistaken deduction that Marcus was Andrew's partner.
- **Ch.28 Evolution:** Received urgent care treatment for a jaw contusion and internal cheek stitches. Protected Justin by telling Vice Principal Davies he slipped on spilled milk and hit a bench, preventing battery charges and expulsion. Maintained radio silence in the group chat while recovering at home.

### Zack Chen
- Third POV character (Chapters 13–16, 20–25). Andrew's partner from Westbrook Prep. Swimmer (200 IM). Parents survived the gala. Chinese-American. Attends a different school from West Valley.
- **Core Personality:** The most grounded of the three POV characters. Practical, steady, and direct. He processes emotions the way an athlete processes a loss — feels it, sits with it, moves on. He doesn't dramatize or over-analyze. He just deals with things.
- **Ch.20-26 Evolution:** Impersonated Justin online as a fragile coping mechanism after stepping aside. Connected with Wade Richardson in Pacific Palisades, then realized pretending to be someone else was an illusion. Reconciled with Andrew at a 24-hour pancake diner after Andrew revealed his handwritten notebook and vulnerabilities. Fully restored their relationship over an uninterrupted long weekend.
- **Ch.27 Evolution:** Met Andrew after Monday swim practice at the Westside Aquatic Center; provided calm, unconditional presence and reassurance without demanding explanations or judgment.
- **Voice Distinction:** Zack's internal register allows occasional profanity (shit, damn, hell) — casual, not aggressive. He swears the way normal teenagers swear. Physical awareness dominant (notices temperature, grip strength, spatial distance).
- **MBTI:** ISTP


### Ethan Hayes
- Andrew's older brother, 23-24. Dropped out of college to take legal custody after their parents' death. Lives with Andrew in a fourteenth-floor penthouse. Works from home — running some kind of business/venture (multiple phones, legal pad, laptop with spreadsheets). Calm, competent, clearly successful. Not a struggling guardian — a young professional who took on a responsibility and handles it with the same efficiency he applies to everything else.
- **Ch.13-14 Role:** Called by Andrew during the gala entrapment. Made the deliberate decision to tell Andrew their parents were "fine" when they were not — managing terrible information in real time. This lie (or mercy) is one of the novel's heaviest moments.

### Principal Nakamura
- School principal. Patient, uses silence as a tool. Chose Justin for unclear reasons (maybe sees something in him Justin doesn't see in himself).


### Mr. Reeves
- World History teacher. The type to announce group projects with dangerous energy.
