# Character Profiles

## Main Characters

### Justin Herrera (Narrator)
- **Age / Grade:** 16–17, Junior
- **Appearance:** Not heavily described (first-person narrator). Latino surname. Casual dresser.
- **Core Personality:** Sarcastic, socially comfortable, slightly lazy but self-aware. Reluctantly does the right thing when pushed. More observant than he lets on.
- **Core Values:** Loyalty to friends, authenticity, low tolerance for pretension
- **Greatest Fear:** Being trapped in something he didn't choose (whether a role, a feeling, or a responsibility)
- **Fatal Flaw:** Avoidance — he deflects with humor and passivity rather than engaging with things that unsettle him
- **Inner Desire:** To be genuinely known, though he'd never say that out loud
- **Background:** Out as gay — treats it as a background fact, not a defining storyline. Average student (C+ to low B), standard-level classes. Solid friend group. Dad left when Justin was nine; mom works doubles. House is often empty when he gets home. Lives in LA.
- **Speech Pattern:** Short sentences, casual contractions, occasional dry humor. Doesn't monologue — thinks in fragments.
- **MBTI:** ISFP

### Andrew Hayes
- **Age / Grade:** 16–17, Junior (transferred at start of this semester)
- **Appearance:** Dark hair, slightly messy in an intentional way. Dark brown eyes. Wears plain, clean clothes (black jacket, white shirt). Nothing flashy. Carries himself with unusual stillness and self-possession.
- **Core Personality:** Presents as confident, bold, slightly untouchable. One-on-one: cold, minimal, unreadable. In a crowd: magnetic. This is a reinvention — at his old school he was isolated with poor relationships.
- **Core Values:** Unknown to other characters at this stage. Internally: self-sufficiency, never being vulnerable again
- **Greatest Fear:** Returning to who he was before — weak, isolated, pitied
- **Fatal Flaw:** His reinvented persona is a fortress, not a home. He cannot sustain it without eventually cracking.
- **Inner Desire:** Connection, though he's buried it so deep he may not know it exists
- **Background:** Transferred from Westbrook Preparatory (private school, Brentwood area). Lost both parents over the summer. At his old school: isolated, poor relationships with everyone. Now enrolled in all AP-level courses. Academically exceptional. The specifics of his parents' death and his transformation should surface slowly through behavior, not exposition.
- **Speech Pattern:** Minimal. Functional. One-word or one-sentence responses. Never volunteers information. Not rude — just absent. Speaks like someone who has decided that words are tools, not bridges.
- **MBTI:** INTJ

## Supporting Cast

### Dylan
- Justin's close friend. Joker of the group. Teases Justin constantly. Likely the first to notice if Justin starts acting differently around Andrew. Quick-witted, not mean-spirited.

### Samira
- Part of Justin's friend group. Observant, plugged into the Instagram gossip network. More thoughtful than Dylan — notices things but doesn't always comment.

### Tyler (TylerMoss)
- Friend of Justin's. Straightforward, says what's on his mind without overthinking it. Asks the obvious questions that others skip past. Not the sharpest reader of subtext, but not oblivious either — he just doesn't complicate things. Reliable presence in the group.
- **Ch.8 Evolution:** Marcus identifies Tyler as the only other "non-performer" in the friend group. Marcus is honest on purpose; Tyler is "honest by accident." Tyler has a talent for accidentally saying the right thing in the wrong words (e.g., comparing Andrew's behavior to posting song lyrics as captions). He's also the only one willing to call Marcus out directly ("you literally gave him Justin's number"), which Marcus respects even if he ignores it.

### Becca (BeccaLin)
- Active in the Instagram chat. Empathetic — she was the one who pointed out Andrew's parents died. Not necessarily close to Justin but in the same orbit.

### Marcus (MarcusW)
- Justin's friend. Second POV character (Chapters 7–12). Blunt, principled, extremely observant. Prefers people who say what they mean. Keeps his word because he gave it, not out of loyalty or affection. Has a dry, understated voice. Sees more than he lets on but doesn't push unless it matters to him. Currently holds the secret that he gave Andrew Justin's number.
- **Ch.10–11 Evolution:** Developed feelings for Andrew — illogical, unexplainable, kept completely to himself. Was directly rejected by Andrew in Government class (Ch. 11). Carries this alongside the phone number secret. Punched by Justin in Ch. 12 for reasons he doesn't understand.

### Zack Chen
- Third POV character (Chapters 13–16). Andrew's partner from Westbrook Prep. Swimmer (200 IM). Parents survived the gala. Chinese-American. Attends a different school from West Valley.
- **Core Personality:** The most grounded of the three POV characters. Not emotionally sensitive the way Justin is (Justin spirals, overthinks, changes his shirt twice). Not analytically nerdy the way Marcus is (Marcus deconstructs everything into systems and observations). Zack is practical, steady, and direct. He processes emotions the way an athlete processes a loss — feels it, sits with it, moves on. He doesn't dramatize. He doesn't over-analyze. He just deals with things. His mother says his best quality is listening, but what she really means is that Zack absorbs without overreacting. He's the guy who pulls you out of a collapsed building and then asks if you're cold.
- **Voice Distinction:** Zack's internal register allows occasional profanity (shit, damn, hell) — casual, not aggressive. He swears the way normal teenagers swear, which is to say: frequently enough that it's unremarkable. This separates him from Justin (who is too self-conscious to swear in narration) and Marcus (who is too precise). Zack's narration is less literary than either — shorter observations, fewer metaphors, more physical awareness (he notices temperature, posture, grip strength, spatial distance). He thinks like someone who lives in his body, not in his head.
- **Background:** Attended Westbrook Prep with Andrew but they never spoke — Andrew was the invisible kid. They were thrown together at a charity gala that was attacked (shooting + explosion). Zack pulled Andrew from under a collapsed column, and they spent a full day and night trapped together. This shared survival formed a deep bond that became a relationship.
- **Key Actions:** Gave Andrew the Tiffany bracelet (the one Justin noticed in Ch. 4). Gave Andrew explicit permission to pursue Justin when he recognized Andrew's growing feelings. Stepped aside voluntarily — not from lack of caring, but because holding on wasn't practical. Zack doesn't do noble sacrifice as performance; he does it because the alternative (holding on, watching Andrew suffocate) would have been worse, and he could see that clearly because he's not the kind of person who lets emotion cloud the obvious.
- **Speech Pattern:** More casual than Marcus, less fragmented than Justin. Shorter internal sentences. Physical metaphors over abstract ones. Doesn't perform his observations (unlike Marcus) or narrate his anxiety spirals (unlike Justin). When something hurts, he notes it and keeps going.
- **MBTI:** ISTP

### Ethan Hayes
- Andrew's older brother, 23-24. Dropped out of college to take legal custody after their parents' death. Lives with Andrew in a fourteenth-floor penthouse. Works from home — running some kind of business/venture (multiple phones, legal pad, laptop with spreadsheets). Calm, competent, clearly successful. Not a struggling guardian — a young professional who took on a responsibility and handles it with the same efficiency he applies to everything else.
- **Ch.13-14 Role:** Called by Andrew during the gala entrapment. Made the deliberate decision to tell Andrew their parents were "fine" when they were not — managing terrible information in real time. This lie (or mercy) is one of the novel's heaviest moments.

### Principal Nakamura
- School principal. Patient, uses silence as a tool. Chose Justin for unclear reasons (maybe sees something in him Justin doesn't see in himself).


### Mr. Reeves
- World History teacher. The type to announce group projects with dangerous energy.
